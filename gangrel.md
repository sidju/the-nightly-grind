# Gangrel

#lore #clans

The Gangrel are the closest to the Beast of any clan. Where others manage their
feral nature, the Gangrel have made a kind of peace with it — or at least an
honest acknowledgement of what they are. They are wanderers and predators, more
at home in the wild or the margins of society than in courts and drawing rooms.
Other Kindred sometimes underestimate them for this. That is usually a mistake.

Their disciplines are:

- [Animalism](animalism.md)
- [Protean](protean.md)
- [Potence](potence.md)

## Weakness

When a Gangrel frenzies, the Beast slips through in a physical sense. Animal
features manifest during frenzy — fur, claws, eyes that catch the dark — and
unlike the controlled changes of Protean, these do not simply revert. They fade
slowly over subsequent nights, leaving the Gangrel visibly marked by what their
Beast did and how long it ran.

## History

The Gangrel have no great courts or covenant leanings in their history, no
cultural tradition of politics or scholarship. What they have is survival. They
have outlasted domains that ignored them, navigated territories that killed
others, and persisted through centuries where more organised Kindred rose and
fell. Their elders tend to be quietly formidable in a way that surprises those
who only knew them young.
