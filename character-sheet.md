# Character Sheet breakdown

#index #rules

This page explains all values that normally appear on a character sheet. (And
contains a simple example character sheet at the bottom.)

## [HP](hp.md)

[HP](hp.md) is the classical stat for how much damage a character is able to
endure before falling in combat. See [the HP page](hp.md) for more details.

## Ability scores

The intent is that the three _Ability_ scores define skill in the three main
aspects of the game. Their distribution describes a character's skills; focused
on one for an expert and spread out for more flexible characters.

The scores are the following:

- [Physical Ability (PA)](physical-ability.md)
- [Mental Ability (MA)](mental-ability.md)
- [Organizational Ability (OA)](organizational-ability.md)

## Humanity (Hu)

The humanity stat describes how humane a character is on a scale from 1 to 10.
What these values mean and how one risks losing humanity is described in
[the Humanity page](humanity.md).

## [Blood Potency](blood-potency.md) (BP)

[Blood Potency](blood-potency.md) is the strength of your vampiric blood. The
effects of this can be seen in [the dedicated page](blood-potency.md).

## Powers

Among vampires these are often called powers or
[powers of the blood](powers-of-the-blood.md). They are techniques through which
blood can be used to influence the self or others.

See the [Powers of the Blood page](powers-of-the-blood.md) for a full listing or
the page for your character's [clan](clans.md) to see which ones are immediately
available for them.

## [Influences](influences.md)

[Influences](influences.md) allow vampires to affect human society, shaping the
world to suit their purposes. For a listing of the influences see the
[Influences page](influences.md), for a description of how influencing human
society works see the [Influence System page](influence-system.md).

## [Investments](influence-system.md)

[Investments](influence-system.md) are concrete benefits built from your sphere
of influence. You may have one per 2 [OA](organizational-ability.md) (round
down).

## Calculated values

Optionally you are recommended to write down some calculated values:

- Unarmed Melee Damage: [PA](physical-ability.md) / 3 + levels in
  [Potence](potence.md)
- Significant [Weapon Bonus](equipment.md#melee-weapons): Bonus a weapon needs
  to provide to raise your damage by 1, which it does when your
  [PA](physical-ability.md) + bonus is evenly divisible by 3.
