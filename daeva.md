# Daeva

#lore #clans

The Daeva are creatures of passion and desire. Their blood runs hot by undead
standards, pulling them toward obsession — with people, with sensations, with
the hunt. They are seductive and magnetic, and they know it. Whether this makes
them charming or dangerous depends entirely on the individual, and often it is
both.

Their [powers](powers-of-the-blood.md) are:

- **[Presence](presence.md)**
- [Celerity](celerity.md)
- [Potence](potence.md)

## Weakness

The Daeva's passion turns against them through their feeding. When a Daeva feeds
repeatedly from the same victim, they begin to develop an obsession with that
person — thinking about them, seeking them out, prioritising their wellbeing in
ways that can compromise judgement and self-interest. The deeper the feeding
history, the stronger the pull.

## History

The Daeva have existed wherever beauty and power intersect, which is to say
everywhere. Courts, theatres, salons, anywhere humans gather to be seen — the
Daeva have always found a home. Their internal culture tends toward the
dramatic, and their history is full of intense relationships, bitter betrayals,
and spectacular rises and falls.
