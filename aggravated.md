# Aggravated Damage

#rules

Aggravated damage is not merely physical injury — it is a poisoning, an
infection of the Kindred body by a harmful spirit or essence. The wound carries
something of its source with it, and that essence resists the body's attempts to
expel it. Those who look closely at the wounds of an affected Kindred can
glimpse that essence still present within them — for example the glow of embers
or a shimmer of sunlight.

The most common sources are fire and sunlight, whose essence is fundamentally
hostile to the undead body. Other supernatural effects can also cause it,
notably the black vitae poison of
[Protean — Horrid Aspect](protean.md#2-horrid-aspect).

## Effect

While a Kindred carries aggravated damage they heal at only 1 HP per night,
regardless of [Blood Potency](blood-potency.md). The poisoning does not leave
them until they have fully healed — only once all damage is gone can the body
finally push out the hostile essence.
