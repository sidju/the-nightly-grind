# Mekhet

#lore #clans

The Mekhet are creatures of shadow and perception. Quick, subtle, and observant,
they are drawn toward secrets — gathering them, keeping them, and occasionally
deploying them with precision. They are not a flashy clan. They are the ones who
already know what you are about to say.

Their [powers](powers-of-the-blood.md) are:

- **[Auspex](auspex.md)**
- [Celerity](celerity.md)
- [Obfuscation](obfuscation.md)

## Weakness

The Mekhet's connection to shadow makes them more vulnerable to what destroys
it. They take [aggravated](aggravated.md) damage from sunlight and fire as all
Kindred do, but suffer it more acutely — the essence of those sources cuts
deeper into their blood. They also fall into torpor more readily than other
clans when their [HP](hp.md) is reduced.

## History

The Mekhet have been present in positions of quiet influence throughout Kindred
history, rarely at the head of things but rarely far from the ear of whoever is.
Their culture places high value on knowledge and discretion, and their elders
are among the most extensively informed Kindred in any domain they inhabit. They
are disproportionately represented in the [Ordo Dracul](ordo-dracul.md) and
among the unaligned, where their preference for operating without obligation
serves them well.
