# Clans

#index #lore #rules

A clan is the vampiric lineage a Kindred is born into at their embrace. It is
not chosen — it is inherited from the sire, passed down through the blood. It
shapes the Kindred's body, instincts, and weaknesses from the moment of death.

Each clan carries two things universally: a set of disciplines that come
naturally to its members, and a unique weakness — a flaw woven into the blood
itself that no power or practice can fully overcome.

Members of a clan often feel a natural kinship with one another, but there is no
inherent loyalty owed to one's clan. Kindred are individuals first, and whatever
loyalty they extend to their clanmates is a product of their own experience and
character.
